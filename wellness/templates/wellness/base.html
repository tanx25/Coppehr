About Wellness

The Wellness Program's mission is to promote wellness for the Center on Aging's patients. Inspired by those around him, Dr. Ron Adelman conceptualized a platform where patients could feel less isolated by making social connections with one another and providers. By offering educational programs on food and nutrition, exercise, cultural enrichment, and more, we hope participants will meet new people, make new friends, and learn new things.
